# P1 — world-space arm supports

Complete arm chains and forearm stumps use independent recovery and plant states. The recovery duration uses v1 step swing timing, the stance duration provisionally uses the v1 hop flight duration. Plant XZ remains fixed while ground height is resampled. After the base prone pose and ground correction, arm IK resolves against the world target with an upward elbow pole; penetrating candidates are rejected. Only a reachable planted endpoint within 2 mm of the target can authorize commanded translation. One usable arm pauses during recovery and uses a provisional 1.35 stance multiplier; two arms may alternate support. Collision corrections can still move roots independently. No friction impulse, exact contact forces, or swept terrain yet. Upper-arm-only stumps, head and torso retain their previous phase-based motion. On steep ground or large collision displacement an anchor may temporarily be unreachable and must replant. HUD shows valid planted anchors; tests check fixed world positions, recovery pause, lengths, stump and snapshot ownership. Benchmark now includes 8 legless patients alongside intact baseline.
