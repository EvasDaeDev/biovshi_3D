Transitional falling, temporary knockdown and terminal state remain unable to attack. A change between upright and prone cancels the previous strike. Kicks still require standing on two complete legs. Prone uses the same fixed phases, real swept contact and damage path, but separate ground-aware IK / neck articulation. Non-attacking supports and torso stay in the base pose; blocked candidates retain the base geometry rather than lifting or stretching the whole body. Self-propelled crawl is suspended during the strike, including advance of world crawl anchors; external recoil/collision can still move the patient. ## Standing attacker versus crawling victim (26 November 2026)

User reported that a crawling patient could not be hit. Two code-level blockers were found: standing kick selection only considered pelvis/abdomen/legs, excluding reachable head/chest/arms of a ground victim; a moving autonomous attacker skipped every kick preview until speed <= .10 and both feet stopped, while pursuit kept requesting motion. Expanded combat now includes all actually present eligible victim capsules for kick previews against non-upright victims, and prioritizes the leg for upright attackers in that situation. A reachable preview during approach requests braking and completes the current foot swing before starting a strike; it does not deal damage, force a strike or bypass real sweep/terrain/health tests. Failed-preview pursuit does not override the braking command. Manual input, standing-target ordering, two-leg requirement, original nominal lengths, damage and 2D remain unchanged. Transitional victims are also eligible, but attackers in transition still cannot strike. Added isolated contact/absence/range and mixed-posture native duel regressions; new CI required. No local full build, browser run or verified reproduction of the exact user match. If the mixed regression fails, inspect actual root/heading and floor reach before changing other gates.
