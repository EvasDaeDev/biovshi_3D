# P2 — oil loss and impact reaction

Source values from the local v1 code: oil emission interval .5–.7 s, mark limit 2000, dry 18 s (src/config.js, src/render/effects.js), no working limbs → 10–15 s deadline (src/game/loop.js, src/robot/anatomy.js). No invented continuous oil volume or global damage meter. A product cut opens a wound at the still-attached parent endpoint; subsequent parent cuts remove obsolete wounds. Preconfigured diagnostic damage and artificial lab contact fracture do not open product wounds. A remaining upper/lower arm or leg pair counts as a working limb, as in v1; hand/foot are not required for this timer. With wounds and no working limbs the seeded deadline produces oil-loss incapacitation and existing Rapier handoff. Terminal state clears leaks but leaves marks. Independent seeded oil RNG avoids perturbing existing wander. Oil marks use ground sample height and are purely visual, no collider; simple instanced circles are a placeholder for the existing liquid art and future P4 persistent floor. No coolant/prosthetic distinction yet.

ImpactState uses v1 .52 s flinch, .18→1 smooth slowdown, .34/.49 m/s limb/central initial recoil, exp(-7.5 dt) decay (src/robot/anatomy.js, src/robot/locomotion.js). Native 3D root offset directly integrates the decaying velocity rather than copying v1's additional velocity integration; no animated hit twist yet. Blocked knockdown retains its own pose; terminal state clears recoil. Explicit diagnostic hits without attacker recoil backward. Added unit/integration tests and browser check, existing performance benchmark unchanged. New CI pending.
