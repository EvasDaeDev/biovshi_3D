# P1 — longer, slower, coherent strides

User reported short steps and rapid limb cycling. Previous port used v1's .111 s replant swing and optional speed-dependent lead (~.035 m at .9 m/s), unsuitable for upright 3D gait. New native biped timing uses a .50 s step interval / .32 s swing at walk and .36 / .26 s at sprint, alternating one airborne foot, with world-space targets predicted by speed over swing plus half the next step interval. Expected nominal lead at touchdown .225 m walk / .342 m sprint; actual IK and terrain may reduce it. The body is lowered slightly during movement to retain reach. Arm swing and opposite girdle twist follow the foot position rather than an independent fast oscillator. Forward and backward use signed targets; low speeds and stops use displacement threshold, idle recentering is a step rather than teleport. Use actual clamped root travel for biped cadence input. Walk/sprint root speed unchanged. Native 3D cadence and reach are provisional tuning decisions, not copied v1 values.

Arm crawl recovery .30 s and stance .55 s, forward/backward target reach .40 m plus lead, replacing short .111 s recovery and .26 m reach. Contact gating and IK unchanged. One-leg hop and residual head/torso rhythm unchanged. New isolated walk scenario, HUD step count, tests for signed lead, cadence, stride, plant and stopping; existing CI benchmark and browser checks remain. Visual acceptance and post-change measurements pending.
