# P2 — segment health and damage entrypoint

The native DamageState uses v1 source values: 3 HP normally, head 6, pelvis 4 (src/robot/robot.js); per-segment 0.28 s immunity (17 fixed ticks), trauma threshold 24, actual removed HP weighted 1.5 for head, critical amount 2 (src/config.js, src/robot/anatomy.js, src/systems/combat.js). The caller supplies damage amount, no random critical selection yet. Unlike v1 protected spine-0, the three native central torso parts are damageable but remain attached on zero HP and trigger terminal torso-failure. This is a provisional 3D rule, not a 2D rule. Head/neck severing with head loss is terminal in product damage. Explicit pre-damaged headless lab configurations remain available for locomotion testing. Descendant HP disappears without adding trauma. Manual lab contact fracture remains separate and only synchronizes detached HP, not product hits.

LabSimulationCore.applyDamage takes a validated contact request, emits fighter-damaged, detachment(s), then incapacitation with cause, in order. Each severing operation emits one segment-detached event with the entire connected branch in detachedParts, root first. The count still measures removed anatomical segments. Rapier uses one compound body for multi-segment branches, preserving the captured relative geometry. Previously missing descendants are never respawned. On settling, branch geometry stays visible and physical colliders are replaced by walkable cones up to .30 m. A single isolated detached segment retains the previous debris behavior. Joints are fixed in the captured pose for now, not articulated. The same path is used by lab fracture and product damage. No damage from ordinary collisions in this path. Health snapshots are owned copies. One-patient diagnostic mode allows normal/critical hits on a selected segment, queued at fixed ticks, and displays HP/trauma. Tests cover immunity, critical hit, cascading anatomy, causes, overkill/trauma, mobility, reset. Real attack geometry, simultaneous strike batches, critical chance from patient stats, oil leaks, reactions, prosthetic metadata and custom anatomy remain to implement. No runtime dependency on v1. CI and measurements pending.
