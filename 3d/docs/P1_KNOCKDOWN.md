# P1 — collision knockdown and recovery

Temporary state machine (standing / falling / down / rising) in native TypeScript. Lab dynamic balance is enabled in normal rig and isolated collision scenario. Strong root correction from the existing capsule collision solver can trigger a knockdown; smaller corrections additionally require the estimated support margin to be outside the patch or absent. Airborne hops and patients without standing support are excluded. These are bounded geometric heuristics, not measured impulse or dynamic force integration.

Fall .65 s, grounded hold .52 s (v1 reaction duration from src/robot/anatomy.js), rise .85 s, short retrigger cooldown .60 s. Translation/turn commands are blocked until recovery, capsule lengths remain constrained, and damage-related loss of support cancels recovery in favor of existing prone mobility. Pose transitions use the existing forward prone blend, not impact-oriented ragdoll. Events fighter-knocked-down / fighter-recovered are available to later combat/audio/session consumers. HUD balance state and isolated initial-overlap scenario make the feature testable. New tests cover thresholds, transitions, collision, lengths, normal walking; benchmark includes opt-in dynamic balance scenario. Rapier-driven permanently incapacitated bodies and combat-based damage/trauma remain the next functional tasks. Animation polish deferred at owner's request.
