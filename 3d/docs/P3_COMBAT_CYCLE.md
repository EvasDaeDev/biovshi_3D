## P3 AI escape lifetime regression (25 November 2026)

Eight-patient free-for-all can change a patient's selected opponent during a committed escape. The previous `EscapeState` held its goal until timeout/arrival even when a different fighter became the target, or when one of two pincer threats was replaced. The local v1 `src/systems/ai.js` documents cancelling manoeuvres whose justification disappears; the native 3D adaptation now tracks target ID / sorted threat IDs inside `EscapeState`. Deterministic unit regressions confirm cancellation for changed IDs and continued motion when only opponent-list order changes. No combat timeouts, outcome rules, speeds, strike contacts or RNG schedules were changed. Isolated TypeScript/Node checks passed, but full integrated six-case results remain unverified. Next acceptance step is to examine the generated `combat-audit` artifact and address any remaining observed open fights, rather than counting finite time limits as wins.
