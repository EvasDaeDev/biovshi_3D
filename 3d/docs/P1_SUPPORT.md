# P1 — first support and balance increment

Capsule contacts sample axis endpoints and midpoint (one point for a sphere) against the same terrain height used by gait. Clearance <= 2.5 cm is treated as near-ground contact; contact patches are approximated by a tolerance-derived diamond, and the projected support hull is their convex hull. The center of mass is a uniform-density capsule volume weighted estimate, using only currently attached parts. Negative margin means the projected COM is outside this approximate support area; no support gives null. This is not a pressure, friction or dynamic stability calculation. Feet use the actual sphere radius (.13 m) as ground target offset instead of .055 m.

The upright pose now performs a small quasi-static weight transfer toward grounded stance feet (65% of deficit, capped at .055 m) and re-solves leg IK to keep original ankle targets. Airborne hops and prone motion do not receive this correction. Reach limits and collision correction can still cause foot drift; no contact-driven root propulsion, dynamic COM/velocity fall decision or hand anchors yet. Support snapshots are plain independent data; optional HUD overlay shows player contacts, COM and the XZ hull at floor level, not exact 3D surface polygon. All geometry is native TypeScript, no dependency on 2D runtime. Tests check contacts, terrain, hull, single support, air, snapshot ownership and constrained weight transfer. Performance measured in existing CI benchmark; visual acceptance remains pending.
